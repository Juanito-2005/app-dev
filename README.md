# 1899

*1899* is a multilingual German mystery science fiction series created by Jantje Friese and Baran bo Odar. It premiered on Netflix on **November 17, 2022**, and consists of **1 season** with **8 episodes**. The show was praised for its mind-bending plot and immersive atmosphere, though it was cancelled after its first season.

## Premise

> Set in 1899, the series follows a group of European emigrants traveling from Southampton to New York City aboard the steamship **Kerberos**. Their journey takes a dark turn when they encounter the lost ship **Prometheus**, leading to a series of mysterious and supernatural events.

## Main Cast

- **Emily Beecham** as Maura Franklin - a neurologist searching for her brother  
- **Aneurin Barnard** as Daniel Solace - a mysterious man connected to Maura  
- **Andreas Pietschmann** as Eyk Larsen - the troubled captain of Kerberos  
- **Miguel Bernardeau** as Ángel - a wealthy Spaniard  
- **Isabella Wei** as Ling Yi - a young woman from Hong Kong  
- **Yann Gael** as Jérôme - a French stowaway  
- **Rosalie Craig** as Virginia Wilson - a wealthy British woman  

## Why It’s Unique

- Multilingual dialogue with characters from all over Europe  
- Genre blend: mystery, horror, and science fiction  
- Complex, twisting plot with simulation and reality themes  
- Atmospheric visuals and strong performances  

## Fun Fact

The series’ finale reveals that the events aboard the Kerberos are part of a simulation, and the real year is **2099** - not 1899 as everyone believed.
